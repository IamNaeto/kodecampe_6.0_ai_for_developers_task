Global warming is the long-term increase in Earth's average surface temperature due mainly to human-generated greenhouse gases. Major causes include burning fossil fuels, deforestation, agriculture, and industrial activity.

Observed and expected effects include rising temperatures and sea levels, melting glaciers and ice sheets, more severe heatwaves, droughts, floods and wildfires, ocean warming, biodiversity loss, food insecurity, and human-health risks.

Responses include replacing fossil fuels with renewable energy, improving energy efficiency, using sustainable transport and agriculture, protecting and restoring forests, reducing waste, and international cooperation. The Paris Agreement aims to hold warming well below 2 degrees Celsius and pursue efforts to limit it to 1.5 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels.
